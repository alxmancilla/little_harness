# Big Stone Gap (film)

[0] Big Stone Gap is a 2014 American drama romantic comedy film written and directed by Adriana Trigiani and produced by Donna Gigliotti for Altar Identity Studios, a subsidiary of Media Society.
[1]  Based on Trigiani's 2000 best-selling novel of the same name, the story is set in the actual Virginia town of Big Stone Gap circa 1970s.
[2]  The film had its world premiere at the Virginia Film Festival on November 6, 2014.
