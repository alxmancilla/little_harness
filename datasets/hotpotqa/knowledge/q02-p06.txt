# A Kiss for Corliss

[0] A Kiss for Corliss is a 1949 American comedy film directed by Richard Wallace and written by Howard Dimsdale.
[1]  It stars Shirley Temple in her final starring role as well as her final film appearance.
[2]  It is a sequel to the 1945 film "Kiss and Tell".
[3]  "A Kiss for Corliss" was retitled "Almost a Bride" before release and this title appears in the title sequence.
[4]  The film was released on November 25, 1949, by United Artists.
