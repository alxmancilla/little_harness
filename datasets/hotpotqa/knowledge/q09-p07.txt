# Carl B. Stokes United States Courthouse

[0] The Carl B. Stokes Federal Court House Building is a skyscraper located in downtown Cleveland, Ohio.
[1]  It is also known as the Carl B. Stokes Federal Court House Tower, Federal Court House Tower, and the Stokes Tower.
[2]  The 23-story building is 430 ft tall and is located at the corner of Huron Road and Superior Avenue.
[3]  It is currently the fourth tallest United States courthouse in the country.
