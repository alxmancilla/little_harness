# Hounsfield Heights/Briar Hill, Calgary

[0] Hounsfield Heights/Briar Hill is an inner suburban neighbourhood in northwest Calgary, Alberta, Canada.
[1]  Located north of the Hillhurst and West Hillhurst communities, the boundaries of the district are 16th Avenue N (Trans-Canada Highway)to the north; 14th Street W to the east; Lane north of 7th Avenue N to 19th Street W and 8th Avenue N to the south; and Crowchild Trail, 12th Avenue N, Juniper Road, and 22nd Street W to the west.
[2]  Lions Park C-Train station is located within the community.
[3]  The community is built on an escarpment and is popular for its views of downtown to the south and the Rocky Mountains to the west.
