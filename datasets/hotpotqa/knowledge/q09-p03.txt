# Villard (imprint)

[0] Villard, also known as Villard Books, is a publishing imprint of Random House, one of the largest publishing companies in the world.
[1]  It was founded in 1983.
[2]  Villard began as an independent imprint of Random House and is currently a sub-imprint of Ballantine Books, itself an imprint of Random House.
[3]  It was named after a Stanford White brownstone mansion on Madison Avenue that was the home of Random House for twenty years.
