# Verdi, the King of Melody

[0] Giuseppe Verdi, released theatrically in the USA as The Life and Music of Giuseppe Verdi and on video as Verdi, the King of Melody, is a 1953 Italian biographical film starring Pierre Cressoy and directed by Raffaello Matarazzo.
[1]  It is based on adult life events of the composer Giuseppe Verdi.
[2]  The film was a commercial success, grossing over 957 million lire at the Italian box office.
