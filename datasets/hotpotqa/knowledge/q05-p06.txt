# New York Society of Model Engineers

[0] The New York Society of Model Engineers (NYSME) was originally incorporated in 1926 in New York City.
[1]  There are published records that show the Society existed as early as 1905.
[2]  In its early years, the organization moved to and from various locations throughout Manhattan.
[3]  AT that time it was basically a gentlemen's club of members who were interested in all types of model building.
[4]  In 1926 the Society was formalized and incorporated under the laws of the State of New York.
[5]  This was done so that the Society could obtain a permit to use a lake in New York City's Central Park for model motor boat races.
[6]  It was also at this time that the Society began construction of its first Model Railroad " The Union Connecting".
[7]  Over the next twenty years, the Society moved from its original location to two other locations.
[8]  Each move doubling the size of the previous location and of course doubling the size of the model train layout.
[9]  During WW2 many Society members were called to service in the Armed Forces.
[10]  Regrettably, the largest of the layouts had to be dismantled.
[11]  The location of the layout in the basement of the Astor Building was requested for the war effort.
[12]  The dismantling was done with care, with salvaged usable materials going into scrap drives for the War effort.
[13]  As members returned after the War a new location was searched for.
[14]  This led to an invitation from the Lackawanna Railroad to move into their Passenger Terminal in Hoboken,NJ.
[15]  They had the space for what would become the largest model railroad in the world at that time.
[16]  The space?
[17]  Only the ornate waiting room for the recently discontinued ferry boats to 23rd Street in New York City.
[18]  Here the layout was built.
[19]  It was based on the Lackawanna Railroad from Hoboken to Scranton, Pa.
[20]  It was magnificent; from the scale model of the Hoboken Terminal to the soaring Delaware Water Gap.
[21]  During the early-1950s the organization moved to its current location in Carlstadt, New Jersey.
