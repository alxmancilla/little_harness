# Secretary of State for Constitutional Affairs

[0] The office of Secretary of State for Constitutional Affairs was a British Government position, created in 2003.
[1]  Certain functions of the Lord Chancellor which related to the Lord Chancellor's Department were transferred to the Secretary of State.
[2]  At a later date further functions were also transferred to the Secretary of State for Constitutional Affairs from the First Secretary of State, a position within the government held by the Deputy Prime Minister.
