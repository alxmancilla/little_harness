# I Love NY (2015 film)

[0] I Love NY, also known as I Love New Year, is an Indian romantic comedy film directed by Radhika Rao and Vinay Sapru starring Sunny Deol and Kangana Ranaut in lead roles.
[1]  The film is produced by Bhushan Kumar and Krishan Kumar under the banner of Super Cassettes Industries Ltd.
[2]  The film was extensively shot in Mumbai, New York City and Bangkok.
[3]  The main plot was taken from the Russian romantic comedy "The Irony of Fate" (1976).
[4]  After numerous delays, the film released on 10 July 2015.
