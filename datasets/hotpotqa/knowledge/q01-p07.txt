# Adam Collis

[0] Adam Collis is an American filmmaker and actor.
[1]  He attended the Duke University from 1986 to 1990 and the University of California, Los Angeles from 2007 to 2010.
[2]  He also studied cinema at the University of Southern California from 1991 to 1997.
[3]  Collis first work was the assistant director for the Scott Derrickson's short "Love in the Ruins" (1995).
[4]  In 1998, he played "Crankshaft" in Eric Koyanagi's "Hundred Percent".
