# Mick (DJ)

[0] Mick Batyske (known by his stage name Mick, sometimes styled as MICK, and formerly Mick Boogie) is an American DJ and entrepreneur.
[1]  He is an A-list DJ and spun private parties for celebrities including Kanye West, LeBron James, Jay-Z and Will Smith.
[2]  In addition to his mix tape releases, he has performed in venues internationally, including New York City, Dubai, Tokyo, Las Vegas, and Los Angeles.
[3]  As an entrepreneur, he has invested in various start-up companies including Localeur, in which he is also an advisor and consultant.
