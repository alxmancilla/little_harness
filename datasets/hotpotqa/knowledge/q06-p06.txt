# Madtown

[0] Madtown (Hangul: 매드타운 ), often stylized as MADTOWN, is a South Korean boy group formed in 2014 by J. Tune Camp.
[1]  The group consists of Moos, Daewon, Lee Geon, Jota, Heo Jun, Buffy and H.O.
[2]  Their debut album, "Mad Town", was released on October 6, 2014.
[3]  Two of the members, Moos and Buffy, originally debuted as the hip hop duo "Pro C" in 2013.
[4]  Madtown's official fan-base name is Mad-people.
[5]  Starting December 22, 2016, MADTOWN's contract was sold to GNI Entertainment after J. Tune Camp closed.
