# 888 7th Avenue

[0] 888 7th Avenue is a 628 ft (191m) tall modern-style office skyscraper in Midtown Manhattan which was completed in 1969 and has 46 floors.
[1]  Emery Roth & Sons designed the building, which is tied with Central Park Place for the 65th tallest building in New York City.
[2]  It currently carries the Vornado Realty Trust corporate headquarters.
[3]  Previously known as the Arlen Building, its namesake being the company responsible for its construction, Arlen Realty & Development Corporation.
[4]  The Red Eye Grill is located in the building at street level.
