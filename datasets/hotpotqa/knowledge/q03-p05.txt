# Left Behind: The Kids

[0] "Left Behind: The Kids (stylized as LEFT BEHIND >THE KIDS<)" is a series written by Jerry B. Jenkins, Tim LaHaye, and Chris Fabry.
[1]  The series consists of 40 short novels aimed primarily at the young adult market based on the adult series Left Behind also written by Jerry B. Jenkins.
[2]  It follows a core group of teenagers as they experience the rapture and tribulation, based on scriptures found in the Bible, and background plots introduced in the adult novels.
[3]  Like the adult series, the books were published by Tyndale House Publishing, and released over the 7 year period of 1997-2004.
[4]  The series has sold over 11 million copies worldwide.
