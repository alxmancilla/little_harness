# Victoria Hanley

[0] Victoria Hanley is an American young adult fantasy novelist.
[1]  Her first three books, "The Seer And The Sword", "The Healer's Keep" and "The Light Of The Oracle" are companion books to one another.
[2]  Her newest book (released March 2012) is the sequel of a series, called "Indigo Magic", published by Egmont USA.
[3]  She's also published two non-fiction books through Cotton Wood Press; called "Seize the Story: A Handbook For Teens Who Like To Write", and "Wild Ink: A Grownups Guide To Writing Fiction For Teens".
