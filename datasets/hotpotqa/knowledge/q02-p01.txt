# Meet Corliss Archer

[0] Meet Corliss Archer, a program from radio's Golden Age, ran from January 7, 1943 to September 30, 1956.
[1]  Although it was CBS's answer to NBC's popular "A Date with Judy", it was also broadcast by NBC in 1948 as a summer replacement for "The Bob Hope Show".
[2]  From October 3, 1952 to June 26, 1953, it aired on ABC, finally returning to CBS.
[3]  Despite the program's long run, fewer than 24 episodes are known to exist.
