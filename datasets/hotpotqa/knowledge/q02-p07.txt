# Kiss and Tell (1945 film)

[0] Kiss and Tell is a 1945 American comedy film starring then 17-year-old Shirley Temple as Corliss Archer.
[1]  In the film, two teenage girls cause their respective parents much concern when they start to become interested in boys.
[2]  The parents' bickering about which girl is the worse influence causes more problems than it solves.
