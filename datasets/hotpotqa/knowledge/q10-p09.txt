# List of major opera composers

[0] This list provides a guide to opera composers, as determined by their presence on a majority of compiled lists of significant opera composers.
[1]  (See the "Lists Consulted" section for full details.)
[2]  The composers run from Jacopo Peri, who wrote the first ever opera in late 16th century Italy, to John Adams, one of the leading figures in the contemporary operatic world.
[3]  The brief accompanying notes offer an explanation as to why each composer has been considered major.
[4]  Also included is a section about major women opera composers, compiled from the same lists.
[5]  For an introduction to operatic history, see opera.
[6]  The organisation of the list is by birthdate.
