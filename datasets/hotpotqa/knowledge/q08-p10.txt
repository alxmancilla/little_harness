# Hallelujah! I'm a Bum (album)

[0] Hallelujah!
[1]  I'm A Bum is the seventh studio album by Chicago-based rock band Local H.
[2]  Interviews with the band have revealed that it is a concept album based on politics in the United States.
[3]  Although the album was completed in spring of 2012, the band chose to release it in September as to be closer to the 2012 presidential election.
[4]  The album makes frequent use of the sounds of the El Train in Chicago to transition songs.
