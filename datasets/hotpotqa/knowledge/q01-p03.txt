# Woodson, Arkansas

[0] Woodson is a census-designated place (CDP) in Pulaski County, Arkansas, in the United States.
[1]  Its population was 403 at the 2010 census.
[2]  It is part of the Little Rock–North Little Rock–Conway Metropolitan Statistical Area.
[3]  Woodson and its accompanying Woodson Lake and Wood Hollow are the namesake for Ed Wood Sr., a prominent plantation owner, trader, and businessman at the turn of the 20th century.
[4]  Woodson is adjacent to the Wood Plantation, the largest of the plantations own by Ed Wood Sr.
