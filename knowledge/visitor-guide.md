# Aurora Observatory visitor guide

This is fictional information for the harness's retrieval lesson, not a real venue.

## Visiting hours

Aurora Observatory welcomes visitors Tuesday through Saturday, 18:00–22:00 UTC.
It is closed to visitors on Sundays and Mondays.

## Admission and weather

Admission is free, but a reservation is required for the guided telescope session.
Outdoor observing is cancelled during rain or heavy cloud. Indoor exhibits remain
open during the normal visiting hours.
